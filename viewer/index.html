Volume mesh? Extract its exterior first.

Three-dimensional solvers often use tetrahedral/hexahedral volume meshes. Convert supported linear cell meshes into a displayable surface with cardianatomy export-surface before opening the resulting VTK file here.

B

Interpretation boundaries

The built-in model is synthetic illustration only. A rendered mesh or passing geometry check does not prove segmentation accuracy, anatomical correctness, or clinical validity. The viewer does not reconstruct anatomy from DICOM or NIfTI.
